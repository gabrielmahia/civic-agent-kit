# Portfolio fit — rail vs train

## Decision

Record of Power belongs in the Africa leapfrog portfolio **only as reusable infrastructure during incubation**.

The portfolio should build and preserve the **rail**.

An independent public-interest institution should ultimately operate the **train**.

## Rail — appropriate for the existing technology mission

Reusable infrastructure:
- provenance-bearing claim model,
- entity-resolution primitives,
- alias/transliteration support,
- public-money reconciliation,
- Coverage Critic,
- Due-Process Red Team,
- historical backtest harness,
- cross-jurisdiction routing primitives,
- corrections/accountability-memory model,
- open APIs/export standards,
- agent coordination contracts.

These capabilities improve:
- journalism,
- auditing,
- procurement transparency,
- public finance,
- civic technology,
- research reproducibility,
- government data quality.

They are infrastructure.

## Train — should be independently stewarded

Operational/editorial activities:
- choosing which people/cases to investigate,
- receiving confidential sources,
- deciding publication thresholds,
- writing allegations/findings,
- handling right of reply,
- retaining media/legal counsel,
- political-risk decisions,
- source safety,
- fundraising for investigative operations.

Those create editorial/legal/political exposure and should not remain attached to a personal technology portfolio.

## Why governance belongs to technological leapfrogging

Technology cannot create state capacity by itself.

But reusable rails that make:
- procurement inspectable,
- records interoperable,
- outcomes verifiable,
- public claims reproducible,
can reduce the information/control failures that prevent technology from translating into public value.

Therefore the portfolio position is:

> **Better governance is not a new mission. Verifiable public infrastructure is one of the enabling rails for technological development.**

The boundary matters:
- build neutral capability,
- validate it,
- document it,
- hand operational power to legitimate stewards.

## End state

The technical primitives may stay open-source in the wider Africa coordination stack.

The public Record of Power newsroom/institution should live in its own organization, governance, infrastructure and editorial chain.
