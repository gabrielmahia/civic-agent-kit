# Public brand and domain recommendation

## Internal codename
**JICHO** remains useful internally: memorable, Kenyan-rooted, and aligned with the “investigative eye” idea.

## Public-brand caution
Do **not** launch the public platform simply as “Jicho.” In Kenya, “Jicho Pevu” is already strongly associated with Mohamed Ali/KTN investigative journalism. Reusing a near-identical public identity risks confusion, implied affiliation, and brand collision.

## Recommended public name
# Record of Power

Recommended domain to check/register first:
**recordofpower.org**

Why it fits:
- global rather than Kenya-only
- does not presume wrongdoing
- centers durable evidence and public authority
- works for procurement, courts, assets, state violence, commissions, sanctions, corrections and exonerations
- supports the core principle: political victory or jurisdiction change must not erase historical state

Suggested descriptor:
**A source-linked public memory of money, decisions, relationships and outcomes.**

Suggested short tagline:
**Follow the evidence. Preserve the record.**

## Naming boundary
Avoid names already strongly occupied by adjacent projects, including:
- Jicho / Jicho Pevu
- Evidence Atlas
- Integrity Atlas
- Civic Ledger
- CivicProof
- Public Evidence
- Follow the Record

Registrar availability and trademark clearance must still be checked at purchase time; web-search absence is not proof that a domain is unregistered.

## Product hierarchy

**Record of Power** — public website/institution

**JICHO** — internal investigation/reasoning engine

**Coverage Critic** — blind-spot adversary

**Due-Process Red Team** — exculpatory/rival-hypothesis adversary

**Accountability Memory** — append-only evidence/history layer
